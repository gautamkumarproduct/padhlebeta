Studying Online With Other JEE/NEET Aspirants

Coaching-institute batches and library study groups work for a reason that has nothing to do with the material: someone else is sitting there, also not on their phone. Most self-study drifts because there's no one to notice when you drift. PadhleBeta rents out that same effect online — a live room of aspirants on the same 30-minute focus / 3-minute break cycle, with a headcount you can actually see.

What a study room does that a study app doesn't

A to-do list app doesn't care if you close it. A room full of real people, each with their own "studying now" dot lit up next to their name, is a much harder thing to quietly abandon. It's the same mechanism that makes a library or coaching classroom effective — proximity to other people's focus — just moved online for students who don't have one nearby, or who study late at night when nothing else is open.

How it fits into JEE/NEET prep specifically

Physics numericals, organic chemistry mechanisms, NEET biology diagrams — none of it needs a group to solve, but almost all of it benefits from an uninterrupted block of time to sit with. PadhleBeta's rooms are built around exactly that: a synced 30-minute focus block, non-stop Hindi motivational music in the background instead of silence, and a short break where the room can actually talk before locking back into focus.
