About PadhleBeta

PadhleBeta (पढ़ले बेटा) is a free online study room built for JEE, NEET and Board exam students in India. Open a room, and you're instantly studying alongside a group of real people on the same 30-minute focus block — no signup, no fees, no app to install.

It started as a Hindi motivational study-music player and grew into a small study-room product once it became clear that music alone wasn't the thing keeping people at their desk — the sense of studying with someone was. So the room, the synced Pomodoro timer, the break-time chat and the "did you actually focus?" poll all exist for the same reason: to make solo prep feel a little less solo.

How the room is synced

Every room runs on the same clock — a 30-minute focus block followed by a 3-minute break, on repeat. There's no "start timer" button, because everyone in the room is already on the same cycle the moment they join. When the break starts, a quick poll asks whether you actually focused, and chat unlocks for those three minutes before the next block begins.

The music

Non-stop Hindi motivational Bollywood songs by default, with a dedicated Lofi track and Rain ambience for sessions where lyrics would be too distracting. Rain plays automatically the first time you visit.

Is PadhleBeta free?

Yes — completely free, with no account required.

Do I need to sign up?

No login. Just type a name, or tap the random name button if you'd rather stay anonymous, and you're in.

Is this useful for JEE, NEET or Board exam prep?

Yes — it was built for Indian competitive-exam students who study better with company. The rooms, the Hindi motivational lines, and the music are all built around that.
